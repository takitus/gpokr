=== Summary (max 132 chars) ===

Dark mode, live odds HUD, opponent stats & notes, bet-sizing buttons, session tracker, emotes, and hand sharing for gpokr.com.

=== Detailed description ===

GPokr Tools adds the quality-of-life features gpokr.com always needed — built by a player, for players.

🌙 DARK MODE
A complete hand-tuned dark theme for the entire site: the table (with custom dark felt artwork), lobby, leaderboard, forum, profiles, and dialogs. One checkbox, instant, no page reload.

📊 LIVE ODDS HUD
See your hand the way the math sees it, updated as the action unfolds:
• Win equity simulated against the live opponents at your table
• Your made hand, named ("two pair, aces and fours")
• Draw outs and the odds of hitting by the river
• Pot odds with a clear CALL / MARGINAL / FOLD read when facing a bet
Equity even adapts to each opponent's real playing style once enough hands are observed.

👤 OPPONENT STATS & NOTES
Every hand at your table feeds per-player statistics — how loose they play (VPIP), how often they raise (PFR), postflop aggression, showdowns won. A small badge above each avatar shows the numbers; hover for the plain-English breakdown; click to save a private note ("only raises with the nuts").

🎯 BET-SIZING BUTTONS
One-click bet amounts that float right at the bet box: all-in, blind multiples, and pot fractions. Fully customizable — define your own buttons (any multiplier × blind or pot), drag to reorder, place them above or below the field.

📈 SESSION TRACKER
Your stack is recorded after every hand. Open the popup for your session profit/loss and a sparkline of your run.

⌨️ KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS (optional, off by default)
F to fold, C to check/call, number keys to fill your bet-size buttons. Never fires while you're typing in chat.

😀 EMOTES & HAND SHARING
Send emoji reactions that pop over your avatar, and optionally reveal your hole cards to other extension users after a hand ends — once, always, or never. Your choice.

———

PRIVACY
Everything stays on your computer. All settings, statistics, and notes live in local browser storage. Nothing is collected, transmitted, or shared — no servers, no analytics, no accounts. The extension requires only the "storage" permission and runs only on gpokr.com.

FAIR PLAY
GPokr Tools displays information derived from what is already visible on your screen (the game log and table). It never acts for you — no auto-fold, no auto-bet, no automated play of any kind.

This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by GPokr.

Found a bug or have a feature idea? Post in the GPokr forum thread — the extension is actively updated based on player feedback.

Does not collect, transmit, or sell any user data. Check "none of the above" for all data-collection categories and certify the disclosures.
